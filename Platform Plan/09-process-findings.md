# 09 · Findings from Policies & Processes + Practice Manual (reviewed 2026-08-02)

63 documents reviewed across Z:\Practice Manual and Z:\Policies and Processes. This doc records what changes the plan, the validated workflows, and the extracted rules the platform must encode. (Per-document detail available on request; sources are the process docs themselves.)

## 1. Corrections to prior assumptions

1. **There is no tour-booking software.** The "tour-booking system" is **BOOKING FORM.xlsx** on SharePoint (one sheet per FY; clients found via Find & Replace and copy-pasted between sheets). ODIP's trips module replaces a spreadsheet + paper workflow, not an application. Migration = workbook import, not a system export.
2. **Service agreements are Xero quotes.** The branding theme alone determines whether the printout is an SA or an invoice. SAs are completed by hand in Adobe (John's signature image pasted in, NDIS number/dates/contacts typed onto the PDF). Holiday invoicing = converting the accepted SA quote to an invoice ("mark as invoiced") — billed at quoted amounts, not from delivered actuals.
3. **No Brevity API is evidenced anywhere** — including the meeting notes with Brevity's own rep. What exists: built-in Brevity→Xero contact/invoice sync (with duplication problems; recommended direction Brevity→Xero), NDIS claim-file export for PRODA upload, and manual Service Schedule exports. The Brevity connector must be designed file/export-first; an API remains a Phase 0 question to put to Brevity directly.
4. **Rostering is double-entered today**: built in Rosters.xlsx (staff availability tab + staff↔client compatibility tab), re-keyed into Brevity, cross-checked side-by-side every Monday and Friday. Timesheets are also double-entered (Brevity shift approval AND Employment Hero submission, kms in both). ODIP-as-roster-master directly eliminates both.
5. **Claim deadline inconsistency:** the Pricing policy says payment requests within **60 days** of service-booking end; the working PRODA doc says 90 days. Resolve with the current NDIA terms and encode one rule. Similarly, price-guide update timing is described inconsistently (annually 1 July vs "June and December").
6. **Oassist trades under WOW TOURS Pty Ltd** everywhere (invoices WOW####, NDIS reg 4050012017, policies branded WOW TOURS) — the data model carries both identities (already in ORG-* fields).

## 2. Trip lifecycle as practised (validates/extends M5)

Annual cycle: **accommodation availability drives the calendar** — accessible ("fully modified") properties are scarce and booked up to 18 months ahead, *before* trip dates are set. Then: school/public-holiday checks → brochure → Booking Form sheet per FY → Xero items/chart updates → client bookings (copy row from last trip; verify ratio/night-type against Xero) → coded template quote → SA → out-of-pocket invoice (OP reference; item codes: trip code, ANn active night, TIn travel insurance; "Trip cost X – NDIS claim Y" arithmetic typed into descriptions) → pre-trip admin (~2 weeks out) → paper trip folders on the road → post-trip scan/archive (`Archived Tour Docs/YYMMDD+code+days` with Client Deets / Med Deets subfolders) → NDIS claim after the trip.

**Platform implications:** accommodation becomes a first-class planning root (long-lead booking pipeline per property with accessibility tier and seasonal constraints — the Choosing Accommodations matrix becomes reference data); the trip-code grammar and archive naming are preserved; the pre-trip "Checklist of Checklists" (19 items), trip-folder contents list (with per-trip quantities), and post-trip filing steps become task templates; the 2-week client email (pick-up times, packing list, outstanding docs/payments) becomes an automated, tracked communication; the participant-profile-must-be-<12-months rule becomes a validity check that blocks trip readiness.

**T&C rules to encode in trip billing:** out-of-pockets due 90 days pre-travel (or 2 weeks from invoice if booked later); NDIS component claimed after the trip; cancellation fees 100% (0–30 days), 50% (31–60), $100 (61+); $300–$1000 rebooking fee; mandatory travel insurance line; active-night surcharge; 16kg luggage limit; invoice receipt = deemed acceptance if forms unsigned.

## 3. Money flow as practised (validates/extends M10, 05)

Quote (Xero, template-copied, prices manually checked against the price guide) → SA (same quote, themed print + PDF surgery) → service booking in PRODA (agency-managed; keyed by hand with the quote open beside; quantities rounded **up** to whole numbers — a systematic cause of later balance-mismatch rejections) → delivery → invoice (holidays: quote-to-invoice; community access: **clone the client's last paid invoice fortnightly and re-key hours/kms from Brevity**, add day-type lines by memory, split transport into a separate `…TRAN` invoice for some clients, client-specific routing exceptions like State Trustees) → claim (PRODA manual payment requests or hand-built `NDISUPLOADddmmyy.csv` in Excel — despite the NDIS spec itself warning Excel corrupts date/hour formats and recommending an "in-house application", which is exactly what ODIP is) → rejection loop (dominant causes: claim > remaining service-booking balance, duplicates, quantity over approved) → reconciliation (remittance-email archaeology across two shared mailboxes for plan managers; PRODA inbox CSV downloads ticked line-by-line for NDIA; "minor differences" absorbed) → receipting (reactive, email-triggered).

**Platform implications:** ODIP generates compliant bulk CSVs natively; tracks service-booking balances and blocks over-claims; detects duplicates; tracks the 60-day claim window per booking with alerts; auto-generates day-type lines from actual rostered/delivered shifts (killing invoice-cloning); models transport split-outs and per-client routing (plan manager contacts, special payers, branding themes) as structured billing rules instead of tribal knowledge; imports NDIA remittance CSVs and plan-manager remittances for auto-matching; maintains the support-item catalogue from the annual PAPL import and regenerates quote templates from it (killing the stale-template price trap).

## 4. Brevity & workforce reality (validates/extends M2, M4, 05)

Brevity in active use: leads → quotes → SA (mail-merge + online signing) → client conversion, client funding (budget "safety net", **not** portal-linked; expiry discovered reactively via shift-entry errors), Schedule Board rostering (+ service templates, open-shift publishing to the mobile app), staff mobile app (shift details incl. pickup/drop-off, client docs), timesheet approval (kms confirm; Transport = with client, Travel = alone), NDIA claim batches (file → PRODA), plan-managed invoice batches (→ Xero), employee records. Underused: payroll features. Shared admin login, credentials in a Word doc.

Employment Hero: HR master (onboarding, contracts, quals docs, payroll, timesheet submission); kms visible only by hovering per row in reports. Staff training: paper form → scan → re-key into a training Excel. Worker screening: risk-assessed roles register (roles A/B/exempt; per-worker WSC numbers/expiries — with stale and lapsed entries observable) per the Worker Screening policy.

**Platform implications:** the compatibility matrix, availability, WSC/credential expiries and training records become live rostering constraints; ODIP's cutover plan for Brevity must decide per-function (rostering first — it kills the Excel duplicate; claim-file generation may move to ODIP entirely if no API materialises, making "Brevity sync" primarily a client/contact data question); per-user accounts with real audit trails replace shared logins.

## 5. Compliance workflows extracted (validates/extends M9)

- **Incidents:** documented state machine — identify → verbal report → record → manager review → verify-reportable → (24h immediate notification for death/serious injury/abuse/unlawful contact/sexual misconduct, and unauthorised RP with harm; 5-business-day form otherwise for unauthorised RP; 5-day form follows all immediate notifications; ~60-day final report when required) → investigation (senior worker/KMP/external; prescribed report contents) → resolve → review → close with register entry. Portal uses **Notifier** (drafts) / **Approver** (submits) roles — a maker-checker workflow to model. Clock runs from key-personnel awareness. Data-breach → OAIC; worker injury → WorkSafe; crimes → police.
- **Complaints:** receive (any channel, anonymous allowed) → record/acknowledge with expected timeframes → categorise/prioritise → incident-link branch → resolve with procedural fairness → follow up → review → monitor; register with open/closed status reviewed monthly; complaints info re-issued to participants **annually**; escalation to NDIS Commissioner at any time. No hard internal SLAs documented — make SLAs configurable.
- **Restrictive practices:** Vic flow = practitioner-authored PBSP → consent → APO + Senior Practitioner authorisation in **RIDS** (each practice record: intervention type, routine/PRN, description incl. drug/dose/route or method + time window, linked to outlet and BSP) → NDIS lodgement. Commission reporting: **monthly** for routine and PRN use (PRN needs date, start/end time, incident reference), **monthly NIL reports** when a plan includes RP but none used, **fortnightly** during short-term state approvals. Unauthorised use → reportable incident + interim BSP within 1 month + comprehensive within 6 months. PBSP review ≤ 12 months.
- **Participant lifecycle:** entry assessment → written outcome (4 types) → initial meeting (induction pack, consents: info-sharing/NDIA/prior providers) → **signed SA is a hard gate before service starts** → assessments and plans **within 3 months** → quarterly reviews (risk/health/budget) and 12-monthly overall review → exit (4 weeks notice; provider-initiated needs documented reasons; death branch with reportable-incident hook and notification checklist).
- **Records:** 7-year retention (incidents, complaints, RP records, service records incl. per-instance signed support logs), 5-year financial; NDIA document requests within 30 days; FOI 7 days; annual Terms of Business renewal.

## 6. The deadline engine (new cross-cutting requirement)

The single most repeated failure mode across every domain is calendar-driven obligations tracked by hand (Daily Plan.xlsx, Due Date spreadsheet with corrupt dates, in-trays, memory). ODIP needs one generic **deadline/obligation engine** — typed obligations with source, due date, owner, escalation, and evidence link — covering at minimum: claim windows (60d from service-booking end), incident timers (24h/5d/60d), RP reporting cadences (monthly, NIL, fortnightly), BSP deadlines (interim 1m, comprehensive 6m, review 12m), participant plan/agreement expiries, participant-profile 12-month validity, onboarding 3-month plan completion, quarterly/annual reviews, annual complaints-info re-issue, policy/register review schedule, WSC/credential expiries, vehicle servicing, out-of-pocket payment terms (90 days pre-travel), and the annual PAPL price update.
