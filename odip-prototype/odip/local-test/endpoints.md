# ODIP.Api — Endpoint Inventory

Generated by reading every file in `backend\Odip.Api\Controllers\`. All routes are
prefixed as shown per controller (already includes `api/v1/...`). "Auth" column
shows the effective requirement (class-level `[Authorize]`/`[Authorize(Roles=...)]`
combined with any action-level override — action-level always wins when present).
`Authenticated` = any valid JWT, any role. Role lists are exactly as written in the
`[Authorize(Roles = "...")]` attributes.

Legend: **SA**=SuperAdmin, **A**=Admin, **C**=Coordinator, **SW**=SupportWorker,
**RO**=ReadOnly. Note: `ReadOnlyMiddleware` additionally blocks POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE
for role **RO** globally regardless of what's listed below.

## AuthController — `api/v1/auth` (no class-level `[Authorize]`)

## ServiceAgreementDraftsController — `api/v1/participants/{participantId:guid}/service-agreement-drafts` — class: `[Authorize]`
- GET `` — A, C, SA (a draft carries money, which SupportWorker and ReadOnly never see) — the participant's draft revisions, newest first: the newest in full, every older one as a SUMMARY (`isSummary` true, `blockCount`, `lineCount`, `total`, `caveats`: no blocks, lines or pricing; read one in full from `/{id}` when it is opened). A revision in full made with the plan builder carries its `blocks` (each the pricing engine's block and the worker `requirements`, and `unreadable: true` with an empty block when the stored text can no longer be read), the `lines` the server generated from them (band, unit, `total`, occurrences, flags, catalogue provenance) and `pricing` (what the engine answered when it was saved: totals, issues, notices, holiday occurrences, open questions, no per-occurrence lines). A draft typed by hand before the builder has no blocks and no pricing and reads as it always did. `caveats` are sentences saying what a total leaves out (shifts not priced, lines to review, provisional rates); empty for a revision priced in full. Restricted to Admin, Coordinator and SuperAdmin like the writes: it returns unit prices, totals and the pricing answer, and money is never visible to SupportWorker or ReadOnly (the same as Claims and Billing; a role that is refused gets a 403). Every revision (a summary too) carries `approval` once somebody has approved it for rostering (phase D; it is not `status`, which stays the template's e-signing state).
- GET `/{id:guid}` — A, C, SA — one revision in full (the same shape as the newest in the list); 404 for a revision of another participant or tenant.
- POST `` — A, C, SA — save a new revision (version N+1; a revision is never edited, but the same transaction deletes the participant's OLDER revisions that have no approval, no roster pattern made from them and no signing snapshot, with their blocks and lines, so version numbers can skip; an approved, rostered or signed revision is always kept; a refused save, a 409 and a failed save delete nothing). From `blocks`: the server prices them through the plan pricing engine with the caller's tenant's settings and stores the lines, so the client sends no price and no code; a block the engine cannot price (invalid input, a registration group not held, legacy STA) refuses the save and every reason is in `errors`; Review flags and unpriced bands are saved and shown. From hand-typed `lines`: kept for callers that predate the builder. Never both. Optional `baseVersion` (the version the working copy started from, 0 for none): if a newer revision has been saved since, nothing is saved and the answer is a 409 with `code: "draft-version-conflict"` and `data.currentVersion` (two saves racing for one version get the same 409, never a 500). Omitting `baseVersion` (an API caller; the screen always sends it) is last-write-wins: nothing is checked against the newest revision, the save becomes the next version, and when the newest revision is unapproved (no approval, no roster pattern, no signing snapshot) it is replaced, so a caller that posts without it can delete another coordinator's newest unapproved save with no 409. A save with blocks takes a permit from the per-organisation plan quote limiter (its own partition, two at once): 429 with `Retry-After: 1` when none is free. `[EnableRateLimiting("api")]`, body capped at 1 MiB. A `representative` with a control character (a NUL, a line break, a tab) is refused with a 400 that says so, and nothing is saved; so is the hand-typed path's free text (a line's `serviceType` and `itemCode`, a `serviceTypes` entry), and `serviceTypes` is limited to 20 entries of at most 100 characters that fit the 4000-character column.
- GET `/{id:guid}/pdf` — A, C, SA — the agreement PDF, rendered on demand from the revision (nothing is stored): a title ("Service agreement"), a line naming the provider from the tenant's `ProviderSettings` (organisation name, ABN grouped as "12 345 678 901", "NDIS registration {number}"; each only when set, no line when none is) and "Prepared {date}" (the revision's creation date in the provider's zone), then the participant's details, a "Schedule of supports" (one row for each support from the stored blocks and pricing: days, time, support, ratio, state, hours a week, cost for the agreement, "Not priced" when nothing was priced, and "$X (part priced)" when the stored pricing left part of that support out; a sentence says it repeats every week from the agreement's start to its end), a "Cost detail (NDIS catalogue prices)" table of the catalogue lines behind the cost (a block-based line prints its band, unit and total) and the template's review sections. It carries no "UNAPPROVED / NOT FOR SIGNING" banner, no draft line at the top of each page (only the page number at the foot) and no revision number, template name or hash; signing is still closed. Downloaded as `Service agreement - {participant} - {agreement start yyyy-MM-dd}.pdf` (letters of any script, digits, spaces and hyphens of the name only, so "José Núñez" keeps its accents; sent as an ASCII `filename` beside the RFC 5987 `filename*=UTF-8''…` with the real letters, and the page saves under the second).
- POST `/signing-snapshots`, POST `/signing-snapshots/{snapshotId:guid}/evidence`, POST `/{id:guid}/demo-journey-simulation` — A, C, SA — unchanged (signing is disabled while the agreement template is unapproved)
- GET `/{id:guid}/approval-preview` — A, C, SA, and only a role in the tenant's `PlanPricingSettings.ApproverRoles` (Admin and Coordinator by default; a SuperAdmin with a tenant context always; else 403) — what approving the revision for rostering would do, with NOTHING done (plan builder phase D): `canApprove`, `alreadyApproved`, `reasons[]` (`code`, `message`, `blockId`, `count`, `firstDate`: the same reasons the approval would refuse for), `patternsToCreate`, `patternsToEnd` (+ `endsFromVersion`, `endsOn`), `shiftsToCreate` (0, with `shiftsNote`, when the participant is not active yet), `oldShiftsRemaining {open, assigned, firstDate, fromVersion}` (only the earlier revisions' shifts a coordinator can still tidy: Draft or Published, dated from the later of this revision's start and the provider's today; `open` means unfilled), `overlappingPatterns[]` (hand-made patterns whose real weekly spans and dates overlap), `horizonEnd` (provider today + the horizon, 56 days by default, held to the agreement end), `topUpEnabled` (whether the daily top-up, `RosterTopUp:Enabled`, is on: the screen promises shifts "added each day" only while it is), and `budgetWarnings[]` (budget phase 3: where the shifts approval would make take a pool past its funding for a period, one entry for each pool and period, as a booking's; a warning only, never a reason to refuse; only the preview asks, and one that cannot be worked out is logged and left out; omitted when there is nothing to say). With it off, `shiftsNote` for a participant who is not active yet says nothing is created for them and to use Generate. A revision that is not there answers as superseded when its participant (this organisation's) has other revisions, because a save deletes the participant's earlier unapproved revision: 200 with `canApprove` false and the reason `Superseded`. A participant with no revisions, or another organisation's, is a 404.
- POST `/{id:guid}/approve` — A, C, SA, and only an approver role as above — body `{ "acknowledgeOverlaps": bool }`. Approves the NEWEST revision for rostering in ONE transaction (separate from e-signing): records who and when (`ServiceAgreementDraftApprovals`, unique per revision, audited), makes the weekly roster patterns of its blocks (one for each listed day and worker: a 2:1 block is two a day), ends the patterns of earlier approved revisions the day before this one starts (no existing shift is changed, cancelled or deleted), and generates the unfilled shifts from the provider's today to today + 56 days when the participant may be rostered (else only the patterns, and the daily top-up makes the shifts later). 200 with the revision (`approval {approvedAt, approvedByName, patternsCreated, patternsEnded, shiftsCreated, horizonEnd, firstShiftDate, topUpEnabled}` (`topUpEnabled` is the setting now, not stored with the approval) and `oldShiftsRemaining`); approving an approved revision again is a 200 with the EXISTING approval and writes nothing; 400 with every reason in `errors` (anything the stored pricing flagged, a hand-typed revision, an ended agreement, a delivery state in another time zone than the provider's, more than 100 patterns, or hand-made overlaps without `acknowledgeOverlaps`); 403 for a role that may not approve; 404; 409 with `code: "draft-superseded"` and `data.currentVersion` when a newer revision exists, or when this revision is gone because a save deleted it (a made-up id of a participant that has revisions answers the same; a participant with none, or another organisation's, is a 404), or when a save deletes it while the approval is being written (the restricting keys refuse the patterns and the approval row, the transaction is rolled back, and nothing is kept); 409 with `code: "roster-busy"` ("Another change to this participant's roster is still running. Wait a moment, then try again.") when the participant's roster lock was not free within its bounded wait (10 seconds by default), with nothing written. `[EnableRateLimiting("api")]`, body capped at 4 KiB. Two approvals at once give one approval (the participant's roster lock, then the unique approval per revision and the unique pattern key).

## RosteringController — `api/v1/rostering` — class: `[Authorize(Roles = "SuperAdmin,Admin,Coordinator")]`
Only the actions plan builder phase D and budget phase 3 changed (the rest of the controller is not listed here).
- POST `/shifts/check` — SA, A, C — the dry run. Budget phase 3 adds the shift's budget findings AFTER the domain conflict check and OUTSIDE its early return for an unfilled shift (a budget is about the participant, not the worker): `BUDGET_APPROACHING` (used at or above the organisation's percentage of what is available) and `BUDGET_OVER` (used already above it), warnings that ask for nothing, and `BUDGET_FORECAST_OVER` (the forecast with this shift is above what is available). Each carries `budget` figures (`poolName`, `periodStart`, `periodEnd`, `available`, `used`, `remaining`, `forecast`, `shiftCost`, `overBy`, `bookedAhead` (booked ahead before this shift, so used + booked ahead + this shift is the forecast), `unpricedShiftCount` (shifts of the period left out of every figure because they could not be priced)) and says them: "Takes Core (flexible) to $8,640.00 of $8,000.00 for 1 Oct – 31 Dec 2026, $640.00 over. This shift: about $292.32." (the en dash has a no-break space each side and a word joiner after it, so a line never splits there; when the period was already over without this shift: " It was already $354.68 over without this shift." comes before the cost; the cost is said on this finding only). A shift that ends at or before its start without `endsNextDay` is a 400 ("The shift must end after it starts. Tick 'Ends the next day' for an overnight shift."; `code: "shift-no-length"`, which the shift and pattern panels recognise it by, with the sentence only as their fallback), before any finding. In Warn mode `BUDGET_FORECAST_OVER` is a warning. Under a hard limit, for a ONE-OFF shift (no pattern link on the saved shift; a new shift is always one-off, and a `shiftPatternId` a create request names is not stored) whose change RAISES the cost in that pool and period (a new shift, an un-cancel, a longer shift, or a move into a tighter period): `Blocking` for a Coordinator, and for an Admin or SuperAdmin a warning with `requiresReason: true`. A shift made from a pattern, an edit that does not raise the cost, a cancel (`status: "Cancelled"` in the body), and a shift that is InProgress, PendingReview or Completed only warn or get no finding. No finding at all when the participant has no plan that has started, the shift lands in no recorded pool or outside the plan's dates, or the estimator cannot price it; in that last case the envelope's `message` is "Budget not checked: {reason}." (not a finding: it blocks nothing and is never stored). The body may carry `status`, the status the shift would be saved with
- POST `/shifts` — SA, A, C — create. Runs the same budget check. A Blocking budget finding is a 422 and nothing is saved. `emergency: true` with the description in `overrideReason` (at least 10 and at most 1,900 trimmed characters, else 400 "Describe the emergency or safety need in at least 10 characters." or "...in at most 1,900 characters.") is accepted past `BUDGET_FORECAST_OVER` in every mode and answers the budget only (any other Blocking finding still refuses): the shift stores `OverrideReason` "Emergency or safety: {description}" and `AcknowledgedFindingCodes` `BUDGET_EMERGENCY`, and ONE `BudgetEmergencyReview` task is raised in the same save (`SourceKey` `budget-emergency:{shiftId}`, idempotent while it is open; once it is Completed or Cancelled a further emergency on the shift raises a fresh task, `:2`, `:3`; title "Review emergency shift over budget: {participant} on {date}"; due the provider's tomorrow; `LinkTo` the participant's week on the roster board; the shift's organisation). A flag with no budget finding to answer is just a save. The server owns the budget codes: a `BUDGET_FORECAST_OVER` or `BUDGET_EMERGENCY` in the request's `acknowledgedFindingCodes` is dropped; `BUDGET_FORECAST_OVER` is stored only for an Admin override that carried a reason
- PUT `/shifts/{id:guid}` — SA, A, C — update. The same check and gate, and the same length 400 (same code) when the times changed or a cancelled shift is set live again (never for a cancel, so a row saved before the rule can still be noted or cancelled); the saved pattern link is kept whatever the request names (it used to cut a pattern shift loose from its pattern). A save that does not decide the budget again (an edit that does not raise the cost, a note) keeps the acknowledgement the shift already has, and its reason when nothing else supplies one
- POST `/shifts/{id:guid}/assign` — SA, A, C — not a budget moment: no budget check, and the shift keeps its over-budget acknowledgement
- Shifts (`ShiftDto`, in the board, create and update) now carry `acknowledgedFindingCodes` (the codes stored; the over-budget marker is read from these) and, for an emergency booking, `budgetReview` (`state` Pending or Reviewed, `recordedAt`, `reviewTaskTitle`, `reviewedOn`, `reviewedBy` (the Admin who completed the review task), from the shift's newest review task)
- GET `/board` (`?weekStart&groupBy`) — SA, A, C — each shift now carries `requirements` (gender preference, driver, skills) when the pattern it was generated from asked for any; informational, nothing checks it. It also carries `fromAgreement` (true) and `sourceDraftVersion` when that pattern was made by an agreement revision (both omitted otherwise; two small queries for the week), so the shift panel needs no read of the pattern. As everywhere in this API a null member is left out of the JSON: an unfilled shift has no `staffId` and no `staffName` at all. The page opens `?date=<Monday>&participant=<id>&unfilled=1` links (the filters are the page's own).
- GET `/patterns` (`?participantId`), GET `/patterns/{id:guid}` — SA, A, C — a pattern an approved agreement made also carries `sourceDraftId`, `sourceBlockKey`, `sourceDraftVersion`, `workerSlot` and `requirements`; a hand-made or demo pattern has none of them. DELETE is unchanged and nothing about an agreement pattern is locked (the page warns that changing it makes the roster differ from the agreement). PUT `/patterns/{id:guid}` answers 409 with "This agreement already has a pattern for that block, day and worker. Edit that one instead, or pick another day." when the new day collides with the partial unique index of the pattern's agreement block (any other constraint failure is not swallowed); an edit changes the days not yet generated and leaves the shifts already made as they are.
- POST `/patterns`, PUT `/patterns/{id:guid}` — SA, A, C — a pattern has to make shifts that have a length (budget phase 3 review): one whose `endTime` is at or before its `startTime` without `endsNextDay` is a 400 with the shift's own sentence ("The shift must end after it starts. Tick 'Ends the next day' for an overnight shift.") and the same `code: "shift-no-length"`, and nothing is saved. A create is always checked; an update only when it changes the times (`startTime`, `endTime` or `endsNextDay`), so a pattern saved before the rule can still be switched off or have its notes edited. The server is the backstop: the pattern panel says it under End time before it asks.
- POST `/patterns/{id:guid}/generate` (`?from&to`) — SA, A, C — the result also carries `budgetWarnings[]` (budget phase 3; the same shape as a booking's): where the shifts just made take a pool past its funding for a period. A warning only, in every mode; omitted when there is nothing to say. Now the shared generator (also used by approval and the daily top-up): it takes the participant's advisory lock on PostgreSQL, copies the pattern's requirements onto each shift, and leaves out a day the pattern's agreement plan skips for a public holiday. Otherwise unchanged: idempotent by (pattern, date), gated by participant readiness. It also records how far the pattern has been generated (`GeneratedThrough`), but only when the window starts no later than the day after the old value, so a window far ahead cannot hide the days between from the daily job; and it answers 409 with the same "roster is still running" words when the participant's roster lock stays busy. When nothing has been recorded on the pattern yet, a window is recorded only if it starts on or before the first day the daily top-up would make: the provider's today, or the pattern's start when that is later (the endpoint reads the provider's today for the pattern's organisation); one that starts later leaves the days before it to the top-up.

## ParticipantInquiriesController — `api/v1/inquiries` — class: `[Authorize]`

## AgreementCheckController — `api/v1/participants/{participantId:guid}/funding` — class: `[Authorize(Roles = "SuperAdmin,Admin,Coordinator")]`
Budget phase 2b: the agreement budget bar's check. Money is on the answer, so every request is admitted to SuperAdmin, Admin and Coordinator and nobody else; a SuperAdmin must have chosen an organisation to view as (400 otherwise). A question and not a gate: nothing is written, and nothing in the answer blocks a save or an approval, in any budget mode.
- POST `/agreement-check` — SA, A, C — prices the agreement IN PROCESS with the plan pricing engine (the same engine, catalogue, holidays and organisation settings as `POST /plan-pricing/quote`) and places every priced line on the participant's current plan with the ledger's own rule: the funding period is the line's service date, the pool is the PACE category of the item it was priced with and how the participant's money is managed. Body: either `{ blocks, periodFrom, periodTo }` (the draft being built) or `{ draftId }` (a saved draft's own blocks and dates), never both. Answer: `hasBudget` (false with no plan running now: the agreement is then not even priced and the answer is only the day, and `noBudgetReason` says why in the Budgets list's three words, `NotRecorded`, `PlanEnded` with `planEnd`, or `NotStarted` with `planStart`: a plan recorded for later, which outranks one that ended), `planId`/`planStart`/`planEnd`, `asOf` (the provider's today), `periodFrom`/`periodTo`, `agreementCost` (everything the engine priced), `pools` (only the pools and periods the agreement touches, in the plan's order; 0 when none) and its sentence-name, "Core" or "Core (plan managed)" when the plan holds two Core pools), `notInARecordedPool` and `outsideThePlan` (shown, never dropped). 400 with every reason in `errors` (neither or both ways of saying what to price, an end before the start, years outside 2000 to 2100 or more than 800 days, more than 200 blocks); ## Summary / testing implications

- **Every** endpoint requires at minimum `[Authorize]` (a valid, non-expired JWT with
  `iss`/`aud` = `Odip`) **except** `POST /api/v1/auth/exchange` (anonymous, rate-limited:
  a 60/5min/IP flood guard plus a lockout after 10 failures — not used by our JWT-minting flow) and implicitly the Swagger UI
  (`/swagger`, Development-only).
- Read (`GET`) endpoints are almost universally open to **any authenticated role**
  (plain `[Authorize]`, no role list) — writes (`POST`/`PUT`/`PATCH`/`DELETE`) are
  almost universally restricted to `Admin,Coordinator,SuperAdmin`.
- `IncidentsController`'s `POST` (create incident) is the **only** write endpoint that
  also allows `SupportWorker`.
- `AdminUsersController` and `TenantsController` are **SuperAdmin-only for everything**,
  including reads.
- `ClaimsController` and `ProviderSettingsController` are restricted at the class level
  to `SuperAdmin,Admin,Coordinator` even for reads (no plain `SupportWorker`/`ReadOnly`
  access at all).
- A JWT with `role=SuperAdmin` (the default `mint-jwt.js` output) passes every
  `[Authorize(Roles=...)]` check in this table and is the only role that can reach
  `AdminUsersController`/`TenantsController`. See `auth-notes.md` for why.
