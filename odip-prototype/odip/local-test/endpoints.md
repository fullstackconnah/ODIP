# ODIP.Api — Endpoint Inventory

Generated by reading every file in `backend\Odip.Api\Controllers\`. All routes are
prefixed as shown per controller (already includes `api/v1/...`). "Auth" column
shows the effective requirement (class-level `[Authorize]`/`[Authorize(Roles=...)]`
combined with any action-level override — action-level always wins when present).
`Authenticated` = any valid JWT, any role. Role lists are exactly as written in the
`[Authorize(Roles = "...")]` attributes.

Legend: **SA**=SuperAdmin, **A**=Admin, **C**=Coordinator, **SW**=SupportWorker,
**RO**=ReadOnly. Note: `ReadOnlyMiddleware` additionally blocks POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE
for role **RO** globally regardless of what's listed below.

## AuthController — `api/v1/auth` (no class-level `[Authorize]`)
- POST `/exchange` — Anonymous (a flood guard of 60 requests / 5 min per IP, and a lockout after 10 FAILED exchanges in 15 min) — exchange a Firebase ID token for an Odip backend JWT (issues `odip_jwt` cookie + returns token in body). A refusal is a 401 whose body's `code` says why (`InvalidToken`, `EmailNotVerified`, `ProviderNotAllowed`, `NoOdipAccount`, `Ambiguous`, `TenantInactive`), or a 429 (`LockedOut`, with `Retry-After`); see the repo-root `docs/runbooks/sign-in-trouble.md` (not `odip-prototype/odip/docs/`)
- POST `/logout` — Authenticated — clears the `odip_jwt` cookie

## PlanPricingController — `api/v1/plan-pricing` — class: `[Authorize(Roles = "SuperAdmin,Admin,Coordinator")]`
INTERNAL (plan builder phase B): the phase C builder screen is the only intended caller; the shapes may change with it. A SuperAdmin must pick an organisation (`X-View-As-Tenant`) first: the settings belong to one tenant.
- POST `/quote` — SA, A, C — price weekly support blocks over an agreement period (body `blocks`, `periodFrom`, `periodTo`, `includeLines`); uses the caller's tenant's pricing settings, the date-effective catalogue and the delivery states' public holidays (synced rows plus the override table); a block with a mistake is an issue in the answer, an unpriceable request is a 400 (including `includeLines` true on an answer of more than 60,000 lines: ask for the totals only); rate limited with the `api` policy and at most 2 in flight per organisation (a third gets a 429 "Try again shortly"); the answer carries `timeBasis` (`tz-database` or `fixed+10:00`)
- GET `/settings` — SA, A, C — the tenant's pricing settings (the owner-approved defaults with `isDefault` true until something is stored)
- PUT `/settings` — SA, A — change pricing settings (only the fields sent change; sending the registration groups confirms them; a per-kilometre rate is at most $5; Coordinator excluded)

## ServiceAgreementDraftsController — `api/v1/participants/{participantId:guid}/service-agreement-drafts` — class: `[Authorize]`
- GET `` — A, C, SA (a draft carries money, which SupportWorker and ReadOnly never see) — the participant's draft revisions, newest first: the newest in full, every older one as a SUMMARY (`isSummary` true, `blockCount`, `lineCount`, `total`, `caveats`: no blocks, lines or pricing; read one in full from `/{id}` when it is opened). A revision in full made with the plan builder carries its `blocks` (each the pricing engine's block and the worker `requirements`, and `unreadable: true` with an empty block when the stored text can no longer be read), the `lines` the server generated from them (band, unit, `total`, occurrences, flags, catalogue provenance) and `pricing` (what the engine answered when it was saved: totals, issues, notices, holiday occurrences, open questions, no per-occurrence lines). A draft typed by hand before the builder has no blocks and no pricing and reads as it always did. `caveats` are sentences saying what a total leaves out (shifts not priced, lines to review, provisional rates); empty for a revision priced in full. Restricted to Admin, Coordinator and SuperAdmin like the writes: it returns unit prices, totals and the pricing answer, and money is never visible to SupportWorker or ReadOnly (the same as Claims and Billing; a role that is refused gets a 403). Every revision (a summary too) carries `approval` once somebody has approved it for rostering (phase D; it is not `status`, which stays the template's e-signing state).
- GET `/{id:guid}` — A, C, SA — one revision in full (the same shape as the newest in the list); 404 for a revision of another participant or tenant.
- POST `` — A, C, SA — save a new revision (version N+1; a revision is never edited). From `blocks`: the server prices them through the plan pricing engine with the caller's tenant's settings and stores the lines, so the client sends no price and no code; a block the engine cannot price (invalid input, a registration group not held, legacy STA) refuses the save and every reason is in `errors`; Review flags and unpriced bands are saved and shown. From hand-typed `lines`: kept for callers that predate the builder. Never both. Optional `baseVersion` (the version the working copy started from, 0 for none): if a newer revision has been saved since, nothing is saved and the answer is a 409 with `code: "draft-version-conflict"` and `data.currentVersion` (two saves racing for one version get the same 409, never a 500). A save with blocks takes a permit from the per-organisation plan quote limiter (its own partition, two at once): 429 with `Retry-After: 1` when none is free. `[EnableRateLimiting("api")]`, body capped at 1 MiB. A `representative` with a control character (a NUL, a line break, a tab) is refused with a 400 that says so, and nothing is saved; so is the hand-typed path's free text (a line's `serviceType` and `itemCode`, a `serviceTypes` entry), and `serviceTypes` is limited to 20 entries of at most 100 characters that fit the 4000-character column.
- GET `/{id:guid}/pdf` — A, C, SA — the unsigned draft PDF (a block-based line prints its band, unit and total)
- POST `/signing-snapshots`, POST `/signing-snapshots/{snapshotId:guid}/evidence`, POST `/{id:guid}/demo-journey-simulation` — A, C, SA — unchanged (signing is disabled while the agreement template is unapproved)
- GET `/{id:guid}/approval-preview` — A, C, SA, and only a role in the tenant's `PlanPricingSettings.ApproverRoles` (Admin and Coordinator by default; a SuperAdmin with a tenant context always; else 403) — what approving the revision for rostering would do, with NOTHING done (plan builder phase D): `canApprove`, `alreadyApproved`, `reasons[]` (`code`, `message`, `blockId`, `count`, `firstDate`: the same reasons the approval would refuse for), `patternsToCreate`, `patternsToEnd` (+ `endsFromVersion`, `endsOn`), `shiftsToCreate` (0, with `shiftsNote`, when the participant is not active yet), `oldShiftsRemaining {open, assigned, firstDate, fromVersion}` (only the earlier revisions' shifts a coordinator can still tidy: Draft or Published, dated from the later of this revision's start and the provider's today; `open` means unfilled), `overlappingPatterns[]` (hand-made patterns whose real weekly spans and dates overlap), `horizonEnd` (provider today + the horizon, 56 days by default, held to the agreement end). A revision of another tenant or participant is a 404.
- POST `/{id:guid}/approve` — A, C, SA, and only an approver role as above — body `{ "acknowledgeOverlaps": bool }`. Approves the NEWEST revision for rostering in ONE transaction (separate from e-signing): records who and when (`ServiceAgreementDraftApprovals`, unique per revision, audited), makes the weekly roster patterns of its blocks (one for each listed day and worker: a 2:1 block is two a day), ends the patterns of earlier approved revisions the day before this one starts (no existing shift is changed, cancelled or deleted), and generates the unfilled shifts from the provider's today to today + 56 days when the participant may be rostered (else only the patterns, and the daily top-up makes the shifts later). 200 with the revision (`approval {approvedAt, approvedByName, patternsCreated, patternsEnded, shiftsCreated, horizonEnd, firstShiftDate}` and `oldShiftsRemaining`); approving an approved revision again is a 200 with the EXISTING approval and writes nothing; 400 with every reason in `errors` (anything the stored pricing flagged, a hand-typed revision, an ended agreement, a delivery state in another time zone than the provider's, more than 100 patterns, or hand-made overlaps without `acknowledgeOverlaps`); 403 for a role that may not approve; 404; 409 with `code: "draft-superseded"` and `data.currentVersion` when a newer revision exists; 409 with `code: "roster-busy"` ("Another change to this participant's roster is still running. Wait a moment, then try again.") when the participant's roster lock was not free within its bounded wait (10 seconds by default), with nothing written. `[EnableRateLimiting("api")]`, body capped at 4 KiB. Two approvals at once give one approval (the participant's roster lock, then the unique approval per revision and the unique pattern key).

## RosteringController — `api/v1/rostering` — class: `[Authorize(Roles = "SuperAdmin,Admin,Coordinator")]`
Only the actions plan builder phase D changed (the rest of the controller is not listed here).
- GET `/board` (`?weekStart&groupBy`) — SA, A, C — each shift now carries `requirements` (gender preference, driver, skills) when the pattern it was generated from asked for any; informational, nothing checks it. The page opens `?date=<Monday>&participant=<id>&unfilled=1` links (the filters are the page's own).
- GET `/patterns` (`?participantId`), GET `/patterns/{id:guid}` — SA, A, C — a pattern an approved agreement made also carries `sourceDraftId`, `sourceBlockKey`, `sourceDraftVersion`, `workerSlot` and `requirements`; a hand-made or demo pattern has none of them. DELETE is unchanged and nothing about an agreement pattern is locked (the page warns that changing it makes the roster differ from the agreement). PUT `/patterns/{id:guid}` answers 409 with "This agreement already has a pattern for that block, day and worker. Edit that one instead, or pick another day." when the new day collides with the partial unique index of the pattern's agreement block (any other constraint failure is not swallowed); an edit changes the days not yet generated and leaves the shifts already made as they are.
- POST `/patterns/{id:guid}/generate` (`?from&to`) — SA, A, C — now the shared generator (also used by approval and the daily top-up): it takes the participant's advisory lock on PostgreSQL, copies the pattern's requirements onto each shift, and leaves out a day the pattern's agreement plan skips for a public holiday. Otherwise unchanged: idempotent by (pattern, date), gated by participant readiness. It also records how far the pattern has been generated (`GeneratedThrough`), but only when the window starts no later than the day after the old value, so a window far ahead cannot hide the days between from the daily job; and it answers 409 with the same "roster is still running" words when the participant's roster lock stays busy.

## Summary / testing implications

- **Every** endpoint requires at minimum `[Authorize]` (a valid, non-expired JWT with
  `iss`/`aud` = `Odip`) **except** `POST /api/v1/auth/exchange` (anonymous, rate-limited:
  a 60/5min/IP flood guard plus a lockout after 10 failures — not used by our JWT-minting flow) and implicitly the Swagger UI
  (`/swagger`, Development-only).
- Read (`GET`) endpoints are almost universally open to **any authenticated role**
  (plain `[Authorize]`, no role list) — writes (`POST`/`PUT`/`PATCH`/`DELETE`) are
  almost universally restricted to `Admin,Coordinator,SuperAdmin`.
- `IncidentsController`'s `POST` (create incident) is the **only** write endpoint that
  also allows `SupportWorker`.
- `AdminUsersController` and `TenantsController` are **SuperAdmin-only for everything**,
  including reads.
- `ClaimsController` and `ProviderSettingsController` are restricted at the class level
  to `SuperAdmin,Admin,Coordinator` even for reads (no plain `SupportWorker`/`ReadOnly`
  access at all).
- A JWT with `role=SuperAdmin` (the default `mint-jwt.js` output) passes every
  `[Authorize(Roles=...)]` check in this table and is the only role that can reach
  `AdminUsersController`/`TenantsController`. See `auth-notes.md` for why.
