    Each trip is one record of its travellers, accommodation, staff and vehicles, and the dashboard counts what is still missing.

  2. 2

    Stay

    50 km

    Reserve accommodation against the beds and bedrooms the trip needs, and get a flag when a property is double-booked.

  3. 3

    Crew

    40 km

    Drag a shift onto a support worker and 18 automatic checks run: an expired worker screening or a double-booked vehicle stops it, and every other warning needs a reason.

  4. 4

    Care

    30 km

    Participant risk alerts are worked out live from each record, and support workers start, finish and note their shifts from a phone.

  5. 5

    Fund and claim

    20 km

    Generate claims from confirmed bookings and preview them, then export the NDIS bulk payment file for NDIA-managed travellers and an invoice PDF for plan- and self-managed ones.

  6. 6

    Stay compliant

    10 km

    A guided incident form with a body map flags any reportable incident not reported within 24 hours, and every change is kept in audit history.
