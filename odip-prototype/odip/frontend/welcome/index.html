8:30 amApprovals

Before

Timesheets, leave requests and incident follow-ups arrive in different places, and each one is chased on its own.

With Bunya Support

One list of what needs a decision: completions, leave, incidents, tasks and compliance items, grouped, with bulk actions to clear several at once.

10:00 amA shift goes unfilled

Before

A gap in the roster that nobody spots until the shift is close.

With Bunya Support

The roster board lays out staff by day and keeps unfilled shifts in a lane of their own, where they can't be missed.

  • Open a shift in a side panel to assign it; checks run as you do, and the serious ones stop it.
  • Save the regular week as shift patterns, and set compatibility rules.
  • Leave requests come in for approval, and availability is recorded for each worker.

11:30 amA new enquiry

Before

An enquiry noted during a call, with intake started somewhere else later.

With Bunya Support

Log the enquiry, start intake from it, and book the intake visit with a calendar invite.

  • The intake wizard takes the participant's details step by step.
  • Carers can fill in a caregiver profile from a link, and their answers wait in a review queue.
  • An onboarding checklist shows what's still to do.

1:00 pmAn agreement to draft

Before

A service agreement written in a document, with the budget worked out separately.

With Bunya Support

Draft the service agreement in steps: set the week pattern and travel, watch the running total, check the draft, then approve it.

  • Funding plans have their own editor, and the budgets page keeps a ledger.
  • A list shows the agreements ending soon, with a renewal review when one comes up.

Agreements are still being built during the alpha, so expect this part to change most.

2:30 pmAn incident on shift

Before

An incident written up on paper or in an email, with the follow-up tracked somewhere else.

With Bunya Support

Built for NDIS compliance work: a stepped incident form with a body map and a restrictive practices step, kept in the same place as the roster.

  • A reportable incident still unreported 24 hours after its record was created is flagged overdue.
  • Each participant has a restrictive practices register, with an authorisation badge.
  • Medications and doses are kept on a register, and risk entries sit on the participant record.
  • Staff credentials show expiry warnings before they lapse.

4:00 pmThe claims file

Before

Finished shifts checked one by one before a claim can be put together.

With Bunya Support

Review finished shifts, approve or return them, or approve a batch at once. Claims are built from approved shifts, with a preview before anything is generated.

  • Export the bulk payment request file and upload it yourself, as you do now.
  • A per-booking invoice when you need one.

On shiftMy shifts on a phone

Before

Shift details passed on by phone, with notes and medication records kept on paper.

With Bunya Support

Support workers see their week of shifts on their phone and work through each one: before, during and finish.

  • The medication dose sheet and shift notes are inside the shift.
  • Witness approvals and leave requests are in the same place.
  • A booked intake visit shows up as a card.

Before day one.

Setting up, and getting your data out again whenever you need it.

  • Import participants, staff and funding plans with the import wizard, and undo an import if it goes wrong.
  • Export all of your organisation's data whenever you want it.
  • Syncs staff from Employment Hero, if you use it.
  • In-app notifications, with each person choosing what they hear about.
  • Changes to key records are kept in an audit trail.
