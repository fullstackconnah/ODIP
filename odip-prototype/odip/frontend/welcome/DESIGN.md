# Design System: Odip Landing (Tourist Drive)

> Recorded 2026-10-02 from the shipped build (`feat/landing-page` at 3647f94c, after two finish-review rounds), not from the plan. Where the build and a review note differ, the build is recorded.
>
> Scope: the public landing page at `/welcome/` only (`frontend/welcome/`: `index.html`, `welcome.css`, `main.ts`, `form.ts`, `motion.ts`, `motion-math.ts`, `frames.ts`, `route-gate.js`, `assets/screens/`, with `capture/` holding the scripts that produced the screens and the `*.test.ts` files beside the code). Sign in always goes to `/login`. This is a different world from the app's "Operational Ledger" in `../../DESIGN.md`; the two share no tokens (see the end of Overview).
>
> Legend: R1 and R2 are the two finish-review rounds (2026-10-01; their notes are not committed, so each rule they produced is restated here); N1 to N6 are the round-2 notes; "pinned" means a test in `page.test.ts`, `motion-math.test.ts`, `form.test.ts`, `frames.test.ts` or `route-gate.test.ts` fails if the rule is broken. Facts that still wait on the owner (the notification address, the collection notice, what early access is, a recapture from real data) are in `RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md`, not here.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Tourist Drive"**

The page is the drive to the holiday. A visitor meets one wide green guide sign over a road that runs into layered ranges, reads the offer in a second, then follows a single route down the page. Six brown tourist signs mark the stops (Plan, Stay, Crew, Care, Fund and claim, Report on time), each opening onto the real product with sample data, until the road ends at the early-access form, marked "Early access 0 km". It is a Persuade surface for operations leads and coordinators at Australian NDIS providers that run supported holidays. The offer has to read as serious software, not as a holiday or transport company, so the sign grammar is restrained, the lettering is plain, the country does the nature work and the real screens do the proving.

The world is Australian road wayfinding with three sign colours and one job each. Green guide signs carry the offer and the destination. White-on-brown tourist signs mark each stop. Blue service signs are used only for Sign in. White Overpass lettering inside a white ring inset from the panel's edge is the whole sign language. The country the road runs through is authored SVG (Pale Slate sky, fern and olive ranges, Pale Sprout paddocks, ochre sandstone, white-trunked gums) and never carries copy.

The page is spacious, signed and staged; the app is dense and tonal, with shadows only on what acts or floats. They share only values copied by hand: the Organic Ledger greens, ink and paper, Manrope for reading and Material Symbols for pictograms. This file owns the landing's tokens, type roles, components and motion. Nothing here is imported into the app, and nothing from the app is imported here.

The page carries its own direction contract: a comment above the doctype in `index.html`, 150 words or fewer, in six labelled blocks (THESIS, OWN-WORLD, STORY, FIRST VIEWPORT, FORM, FINISH) that name the form ("Australian road wayfinding", candidate 3 of 7), the seed key 9680af9b and the staging ("folding volume": a concertina-folded road strip map). Its thesis is the rule everything below serves: the page is the drive to the holiday, one route, six exits and a destination, with no gradient hero, laptop mock-up or icon grid. The comment stays first in the file (pinned) and in the built page (a release check); with it there the page still renders in standards mode (checked in a browser in R1).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Three sign colours, one job each: green is the offer, brown is the route and its stops, blue is Sign in
- A sign is a coloured panel with a white ring inset from its edge, white Overpass lettering and a ring-bordered pictogram box
- One route, six numbered stops counting down 60 km to 10 km, and the form at 0 km
- The only 3D is the concertina: each stop is a paper sheet hinged at its top crease that unfolds (rotateX 58 degrees to flat) as it arrives, on desktop with motion allowed
- Real product screens at natural size, each captioned with a "Sample data" tag; art-directed crops below 1024px
- Authored SVG country; no photographs, no remote images, no icon font
- Open by default: with no JavaScript or with reduced motion every fold is flat, the route fully drawn and the country still; under 1024px the folds stay flat and only the route draws
- Claims only from the confirmed safe list; every number counted from the code

### Named Rules
**The Sign Colour Rule.** Green is the offer and the destination, brown is the route and what sits on it, blue is Sign in. A sign colour never changes job. Blue appears on exactly three links today, all to `/login`.

**The Retroreflective White Rule.** White is a sign material: lettering, rings, rules, pictogram boxes, disc icons and the fields a visitor types into on a sign. It is also the canvas of a proof frame. It is never a page ground.

**The Country Stays Behind Rule.** The scenery is decorative (`aria-hidden`), static under reduced motion and never carries copy. Text sits on signs, paper, sand or a paper-tinted label. The one exception is the tour heading and lede over the wide-desktop backdrop, measured at 9.7:1 or better (R2).

**The Tinted Ground Rule.** No colour in the stylesheet is `#000`. Text is Ledger Ink, shadows take the dark of the surface they fall on, and band grounds are only country colours or Warm Paper.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Overpass (with Arial Narrow, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif), 600 to 800. The sign voice.
**Body Font:** Manrope (with system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif), 400 to 700. The reading voice, continuous with the app's body face.

**Character:** Overpass is lettering on a sign: heavy and white on a dark panel, from the Highway Gothic lineage that Australian road-sign lettering shares. Manrope is a plain, open reader at 17px with 1.6 leading. Headings, names, figures and controls are signs; sentences are reading.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Overpass 800, `clamp(3.75rem, 2.2rem + 6.4vw, 6rem)`, 0.95, -0.02em): the hero name "Odip" only.
- **Headline** (Overpass 700, `clamp(1.5rem, 1rem + 2.1vw, 2.5rem)`, 1.12): the hero tagline, set 17ch wide beside a 4px white rule.
- **Section heading** (Overpass 800, `clamp(1.75rem, 1.15rem + 2.4vw, 2.75rem)`, 1.1, -0.01em): the h2 of the tour, fork and markers bands, at most 20ch wide. The destination's h2 takes the Destination heading step and the footer's two h2s are 1.125rem.
- **Destination heading** (Overpass 800, `clamp(2rem, 1.3rem + 3vw, 3.25rem)`, 1.02): "Request early access" on the form sign.
- **Way name** (Overpass 800, `clamp(1.375rem, 1rem + 1.4vw, 2.125rem)`, 1.1): the two names on the fork sign.
- **Exit CTA** (Overpass 800, `clamp(1.25rem, 1rem + 1.2vw, 1.875rem)`, 1.1): the hero's exit bar; the town sign uses the same size at 700 and 1.25 leading, with its name at 1.25em and 800.
- **Stop label** (Overpass 800, 1.875rem, 1.05, -0.005em): the h3 on each brown stop sign; its km marker is Overpass 600, 1rem, tabular figures.
- **Plate figure and label** (Overpass 800, 2.25rem at 1, and 1.375rem at 1.15): the route-marker plates; the rail shield's number is 1.375rem (1.0625rem below 1024px).

### Named Rules
**The Sign Voice Rule.** Overpass says, Manrope explains. Names, labels, figures and controls are Overpass; sentences, captions, intros, notes and typed text are Manrope. Headings use `text-wrap: balance`, paragraphs `text-wrap: pretty`.

**The Two Ranges Rule.** The only faces are Overpass 600 to 800 and Manrope 400 to 700, requested as two variable ranges from Google Fonts with `display=swap`. Both Latin subsets are preloaded so the hero's lettering is in place before first paint (CLS 0). Adding a face or weight is a new request and a new layout-shift risk.

**The Text Floor Rule.** Page text is never below 13px (0.8125rem). Text inside a proof screen is never below 12px as displayed (R1 fix 6, which is why crops exist). Km markers and odometer figures use tabular numerals.

## Elevation & Depth

Hybrid. The page is grounded by long, offset, negative-spread cast shadows tinted with the dark of the surface they fall on, and it has exactly one authored 3D event, the fold. There are no glows, no neutral grey shadows and no blur on signs.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Sign cast** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.18) inset, 0 22px 30px -18px rgba(12,28,6,0.55)`): every `.sign`; the inset line is the lit top edge.
- **Chip cast** (`0 10px 16px -12px rgba(12,28,6,0.6)`): header and footer chips.
- **Stops bar** (`0 14px 24px -16px rgba(31,45,10,0.5)`).
- **Strip** (`0 40px 60px -36px rgba(31,61,5,0.6)`).
- **Proof frame** (`0 1px 0 #fff inset, 0 24px 40px -24px rgba(31,45,10,0.6)`) and **phone bezel** (`0 24px 40px -22px rgba(31,45,10,0.7)`).
- **Town sign** (`0 26px 34px -22px rgba(80,50,10,0.6)`): warm, because it falls on sand.
- **Fold** (see Components): perspective, a crease light (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.9)`), a 26px crease shade (`rgba(60,50,20,0.16)` to 0) and a shade overlay (`rgba(35,30,10,0)` at 10% to `rgba(35,30,10,0.6)` at 100%) whose opacity is `1 - --unfold`.
- **Focus halo** (`0 0 0 6px #fff` or `0 0 0 6px var(--ink)`): a ring, not depth.

### Named Rules
**The Cast-Shadow Rule.** Shadows fall down and out, long (24px to 60px) with a negative spread, in a tint from the Shadow tints list. Hover lifts at most 2px (stage signs) and presses 1px (chips).

**The One-3D Rule.** The fold is the only perspective on the page: one axis (rotateX at the sheet's top crease), 58 degrees at its peak, 1400px perspective, desktop with motion allowed. Everything else is flat and moves by translation only (the country's drift, the arrow nudges).

## Shapes

The form language is the road sign: a rounded rectangle, a white ring inside it, a pictogram in its own ringed box, a six-sided route shield and a steel post under it.

### The sign (the double ring)
A `.sign` is a coloured panel (`--sign-r`) with a white ring (`--ring` wide) inset by `--ring-gap`. The ring's own corner radius is derived as `radius - gap + 4px`, so it lands at 10 to 12px on every variant. Take the triplet from the variant; do not mix them.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | default | 14px | 7px | 3px |
| hero | the first-viewport guide sign | 18px | 10px | 4px |
| form | the destination sign | 18px | 10px | 4px |
| fork | the category-contrast sign | 16px | 9px | 3px |

| mini | the six stage signs | 10px | 4px | 2px |

The town-entry sign inverts the grammar: paper face, green text, a single 6px green border at 20px radius, no inner ring.

### Pictogram boxes and discs

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** give each sign colour one job: green guide signs carry the offer and destination, brown tourist signs mark the route and its stops, blue service signs appear only on Sign in.
- **Do** build every sign as a coloured panel with a white ring inset by its variant's gap, white Overpass lettering and a ring-bordered pictogram box, taking radius, gap and stroke from the variant table.
- **Do** set anything a sign says (names, labels, figures, controls) in Overpass 600 to 800 and anything read (sentences, captions, intros, typed text) in Manrope 400 to 700.
- **Do** keep the route honest: stops numbered 1 to 6 at 60, 50, 40, 30, 20 and 10 km and the form at 0 km. Change a stop's name, order or pictogram in the stops list, the stage sign, the shield, the fold id, the aria ids, the alt text and `page.test.ts` together.
- **Do** keep the concertina's numbers (smoothstep, 1.0 to 0.45 of the viewport height, 58 degree peak, 1400px perspective, reading line at 60%) and check any change with a four-frame filmstrip at fold-top 98, 85, 70 and 60% of the viewport; the sentence must stay legible at the reading line.
- **Do** make every motion default to open: with no JavaScript or with reduced motion every fold must be flat, the route fully drawn and the country still; under 1024px the folds stay flat whatever the preference.
- **Do** show real screens from the real app at natural size, each in a figure whose caption starts with the "Sample data" tag and says only what the screen shows, with alt text that is a full sentence of what is on screen.
- **Do** serve an art-directed `-m` crop below 1024px for every wide screen, 1x and 2x WebP, with `width` and `height` equal to the file's size, `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"`.

### Accessibility rules
The audience includes people in the disability sector, so accessibility is part of the proof. These are the rules; the next section records what was measured against them.
- **Structure:** one h1 (the hero's "Odip" and its tagline, with a visually hidden ": " between them), `lang="en-AU"`, a skip link as the first focusable element, and landmarks for the header, the two navs ("Site" and "Stops on the route"), main and the footer. Every body band is a section named by its own heading through `aria-labelledby`; the town sign's heading is visually hidden ("Built with Oassist") so the outline stays whole.
- **Names:** every pictogram, the country scenes, the route rail, the posts, the stage numbers, the km plate and the odometer are `aria-hidden` (pictograms are pinned); the header's Request link keeps `aria-label="Request early access"` at every width (pinned); every field has a visible label; the honeypot is `aria-hidden` with `tabindex="-1"`; each fold is named by its h3; the brand link is named "Odip, back to the top".
- **Live changes:** the stops status is a polite live region ("Stop 3 of 6: Crew"); the form status is `role="alert"`, hidden until used, and takes focus when it is the only thing to read; the success panel takes focus; each field's error row is tied to its input with `aria-describedby` and the input carries `aria-invalid`; the form is described by the collection notice.
- **Sideways screens:** a frame that really scrolls is a focusable `role="group"` named from its caption; one that does not scroll has no tab stop, role or hint (pinned).
- **Not colour alone:** errors carry a "!" glyph and text; the current stop carries `aria-current="step"` and an inverted badge; every sign colour carries a label.
- **Targets and focus:** 44px or more on every target; the two-tone 3px ring on every interactive element; anchors and tab stops are never hidden under the sticky header or stops bar.
- **Contrast:** text is 4.5:1 or better on every ground it sits on, signs included; recompute a pair whenever a token or a ground changes (the measured pairs below are recomputable from the tokens).
- **Motion and modes:** reduced motion and no JavaScript leave everything open and static; forced colours keep sign borders; the layout reflows from 320px and survives the WCAG 1.4.12 text-spacing override.
